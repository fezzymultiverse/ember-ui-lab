    Free Mode
    Enable Free Mode (No Keys Needed)

    Chats go through Pollinations.ai, a free third-party service, so your messages leave this phone. It is slower, rate-limited for anonymous use, and has no uptime promise. Keys stay on this phone. Free Mode is never used for background memory work. It is not a crisis line: if you are in danger, contact local emergency services or a crisis line now.

    Media engines

    Picture and PDF providers. They need no key.

    Which engine answers

    Auto tries your engines in the router's order (fastest first) and falls through the rest. Pin an engine to try it first. Pin a model to use only that exact model. If the pinned engine cannot answer, the next one does, and every reply says so.

    Paste keys · auto-sort by name

    Copy your keys from anywhere and tap Paste & detect. Ember works out which key belongs to which provider, saves only the ones you have not added yet, and lists each with its name. Keys stay on this phone.

    Engine keys

    Not needed for Free Mode. Paste your notes if you have keys. Only API keys are pulled out and checked, the notes themselves are never saved. Keys stay on this phone.

    Add one by hand

    More than one key per engine is fine. When one is rate-limited, the next one takes over, then the next engine.

    System integrations
    Proactive AI pings

    Needs the installed app and a key for a supported background engine (current utility routing plan). About every 4 hours Ember wakes in the background and sends one short check-in about your open threads. It stays quiet from 22:00 to 08:00 and right after you have used the app. Only a few topic words and that key are handed to the background task, and both are wiped when you switch this off. The lock screen only shows "Ember pinged you".

    Keep replying in the background

    If you leave Ember while it is still writing a reply, the unfinished request is handed to a background task on the phone, which finishes it and sends a notification with a reply box. It uses the same engines and keys as the app and is cancelled if the app finishes first. The request (including your keys) sits in the app's private storage until it completes, then is deleted. Add-on tools (music, documents, web) do not run in the background.

    Lyrics preview (3 lines, LRCLIB)

    Under the now-playing card, the current line and the next two follow the song when timed lyrics exist; otherwise the opening lines show. The song title, artist, album and length are sent to lrclib.net to find them. Switch off to send nothing.

    Native music widget

    Shows the track playing in Ember's own player in the Android notification shade and on the lock screen, with previous, play/pause and next. It follows Ember's player.

    Voice
    Read replies aloud automatically

    Every reply also has a 🔊 button to read just that one. While it is reading, that button turns red and says Stop: tap it to stop, and it goes back to 🔊 when the voice is done. The 🎤 button is dictation: tap, talk, tap again, and your words are typed into the message box (it uses your Gemini key to turn speech into text). Replies are read with Ember Voice (below) when it is on, and with the native Android text-to-speech as the fallback.

    Lively delivery (questions lift, exclamations speed up)

    The default Android voice is basic. For better ones, install voice data for your language in your phone's text-to-speech settings (Google Speech Services), then pick one here. Voices marked "online" need internet.

    First read-aloud of a session can take a few seconds while the engine spins up — it's warming in the background on app open now, so it should be instant after that.

    Music player

    Ember plays your music itself: upload songs or scan the phone, and the player widget shows above the message box whenever something is playing. No other app needed. The full panel opens from Music Console at the bottom of the left menu, or from the player widget.

    Song identification (Audd.io)

    Setup, once: make an account at dashboard.audd.io and copy the API token. Nothing is sent until you tap Identify in the Music panel, press Identify link, or ask Ember in chat. A short clip (about 12 seconds of the song, as a small WAV) or the link you typed goes to Audd.io with your token. Audd.io names the best match and gives no confidence score. Your allowance, pricing and limits are Audd.io's, not Ember's. The token stays on this phone and is not in backups.

    Music control & documents
    Let Ember control music
    Small freedoms: skip, volume, pick a song on her own

    When on, you can tell Ember in chat to play, pause, skip, change the volume, search, make playlists or say what is playing. It only touches the player when your message asks for it, and everything it does shows in the Ember tab of the Music panel. Ember can see only its own player, not other apps. If you tell it to look something up or scrape a page, it does that with Web Scraping (Settings, Scraping).

    Document Reading

    When on, Ember can read, search and summarise documents in your vault. Requires the EmberDocs bridge (connect via Conduint Files or Bojack Secures). Document text is sent to whichever engine answers, including Free Mode, so leave this off for anything private.

    Agent & API Tools
    Web Search (Tavily + DuckDuckGo)

    Lets Ember search the live web. With a Tavily key below (free plan at tavily.com) you get full results with a short answer, current facts and news. With no key, or if Tavily fails, Ember falls back to DuckDuckGo, which only gives encyclopedia-style answers and related links (no news). Your search words go to Tavily and/or DuckDuckGo. The key stays on this phone and is never put in backups.

    YouTube search & audio download

    Ember can search YouTube and download a video's audio to Music/Ember on your phone, with a progress notification. It only downloads when you ask, and only for links you gave it or that its own search returned. Only save things you have the right to keep. Needs the V1.6.0 APK.

    Vision (read image links)

    Ember can describe an image when you paste an https link to it. The link goes to Pollinations' free image reader, which fetches the picture. Photos from the + menu (Camera / Vision, Upload File) go to the same reader: shrunk to a small JPEG, sent only when you pick one, and only while this switch is on. Ember cannot see photos you have not given it.

    Enable Web Scraping & Saved Sites
    Free reader · plain text, no key needed

    The Free reader needs no key and is tried first: it fetches the page straight from this phone, then falls back to the free r.jina.ai and allorigins readers (those two see the link you read). Providers are then tried in this order, skipping any without a key: Firecrawl, Browserless, ScraperAPI, ScraperBee, Sting, ZenRows, Tavily (Extract), SerpApi (search snippets only), Bright Data (the page itself only when a Web Unlocker zone is set, otherwise search snippets). Several keys for one provider are fine: they take turns and a rejected or used-up key hands over to the next. Octoparse does not read urls: it returns the rows of an Octoparse task you built (add its Task ID below) and runs only when you ask for it by name. SerpApi, Bright Data, Firecrawl, ScraperAPI and ScraperBee keys also back up web search after Tavily and before DuckDuckGo. Page text is sent to whichever chat engine answers, including Free Mode. Keys stay on this phone, are hidden from every error message, are not in backups, and Delete everything removes them.

    Local checks are instant. Provider checks fetch example.com once per provider, so each uses a small amount of that provider's credit.

        Privacy, in plain words

        The short version

        Ember has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics of its own. Your chats, memory, settings, keys and music live on this phone. The maintainer does not receive them. What does leave the phone is listed below, and it only happens when a feature you turned on needs it.

        Be aware: the AI services you use (Groq, Mistral, Gemini, or the free Pollinations service) do receive what you send them, and they have their own rules. Do not type anything into any AI service that you would not be comfortable with that service holding.

        What is stored on this phone

        • Chats and memory (IndexedDB): every message you and Ember sent, chat titles and summaries, and Core Memory (facts and threads the AI learned from non-private chats).
        • Settings and keys (localStorage): your name and "About you" text, persona, switches, and any API keys you pasted (Groq, Mistral, Gemini, Tavily, scraper keys). Keys are stored unencrypted in the app's own storage.
        • Music: songs you uploaded (a private copy inside Ember), playlists, and a small cache of song info (artwork link, album, year, short bio text) so lookups are not repeated.
        • Device panel: your own added quotes. The live readings are not saved.

        Permissions, and why

        • Microphone: voice input. Notifications: reply and music notifications. Music and audio files: scanning and playing songs from the phone. Battery exemption and keep-alive: so Android does not kill the app mid-reply.
        • Location: asked only if you tap "Show Wi-Fi name". Android refuses to reveal a Wi-Fi network's name without it. Ember never reads where you are, and you can switch the permission off in Android settings at any time.
        • Network and Wi-Fi state: to tell whether you are on Wi-Fi, mobile data or a VPN.

        Never stored, by design

        • A crisis flag: the safety level lives only in memory while the app runs.
        • Private chats (🔒): never learned from.
        • Chats during a crisis: never learned from.
        • The AI is told never to save health details, secrets, passwords or keys into memory. That filter is a net, not a guarantee.

        Stored anyway, and worth knowing

        • The text of every message is saved, including one that triggered the safety card.
        • A chat's first message becomes its title.
        • Anyone holding the unlocked phone can open Ember and read it. There is no app lock yet.
        • A backup file you export contains your whole chat history. Treat it as private. Keys are included only if you tick that box.

        Your control

        • Settings, Your data: export a backup, import one, or Delete everything (chats, memory, settings, keys). It cannot touch files you already exported or anything a service kept.
        • Turn tools off (music control, documents, web, YouTube, vision, pings) to stop their traffic.
        • Mark a chat 🔒 Private so nothing is learned from it.
        • Remove a single song or playlist in the Music panel.
